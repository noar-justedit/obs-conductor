OBS CONDUCTOR - USER GUIDE
=========================

Everything the dock does. Installing: see INSTALL.txt.

Colours: green = works, connected, on, running. Red = doesn't work,
disconnected, off, error (and the stop / remove buttons). Orange = you
need to act, warning. Blue = information.

-----------------------------------------------------------------

FIRST LAUNCH

The dock asks whether this PC is the MASTER (plays its own playlists) or a
SLAVE (follows a master). The MASTER / SLAVE buttons at the top change it
later (with a confirmation).

If OBS's WebSocket server is off, an orange banner gives the 3 steps in
OBS (Tools > WebSocket Server Settings > Enable WebSocket server > OK).
OBS Conductor then connects by itself: port and password are read from
OBS's own settings, nothing to type. Needed on the master AND the slaves
(the app drives each PC's OBS through it).

THE DOCK

Header: logo, OBS dot (hover for details), MASTER / SLAVE buttons (the
role of this PC in green), then on the right Home (house icon: on air and
playlist) and Settings (gear).

Bottom of the on-air card: on a master, the transport buttons, then the
padlock (a little apart). On a slave, one row: the MASTER switch, AUTO SYNC,
SYNC, then the padlock.
   STATUS badge (top of the slave's on-air card, hover for details):
     FOLLOWING MASTER (green): this PC follows its master.
     FREE RUN (orange): the MASTER switch is off.
     DISCONNECTED (red): the MASTER switch is on but the master's OBS is
     not reached (the red line under the card says why).
   MASTER switch: on = connected to the master and following it; off =
   free run: this PC disconnects from its master, its OBS keeps the scene
   it is on and nothing from the master (scenes, transitions, media, OBS
   settings, playback) reaches it. SYNC is off in free run.

Padlock = lock mode (red when on): nothing can be changed, played or
stopped - from this dock or any other. Click it again to unlock. Playback
goes on while locked.

Playlist tab (master)
   Top of the on-air card: in the middle the tally (see TALLY); on the
   right the state badge: RUNNING (green) while the playlist plays, IDLE
   (grey) otherwise, and under it a grey badge with the playlist's time
   left / total length and its number of cues (stopped: total and number
   of enabled cues). On a slave the state badge is its STATUS (FOLLOWING
   MASTER / FREE RUN / DISCONNECTED) and the tally sits on its own line
   above.
   On air card     scene on air and its countdown, progress bar, next cue.
                   Buttons:
                     play (green)   from the first enabled cue
                     loop           blue = on: after the last cue, start again
                     stop (red)     OBS stays on the current scene
                     end action     arrow into a window: what happens at the end
                                    when loop is off - stay on the last cue, or
                                    go to a scene (blue when a scene is set)
   Toolbar         playlist list and the "..." menu: new, duplicate, rename,
                   delete, Save (into its file, no question; the first time
                   it asks where), Save to... (another file), Load...
   The cue on air can't be changed (scene, duration, video, transition,
   ACTIVE / SKIPPED, move, remove); "play from here" still works.
   Two lines per scene (the name is never cut):
      line 1   dotted handle (drag to reorder), number, scene name (click
               it to pick another OBS scene), and on the right a small tag:
               ACTIVE (green) = plays, SKIPPED (red) = jumped over.
               Click the tag to switch.
               A cue switched off is skipped, also live (1, [2 off], 3 plays
               1 then 3) and doesn't count in the total.
      line 2   play from this cue, duration in seconds:
                 grey   = the default scene duration (Settings); changing
                          the default changes all these cues
                 white  = chosen for this cue, or taken from a video
                 Click it: menu with Default, usual durations (5s to 60s),
                 Custom (type any, decimals allowed, Enter) and Auto-detect:
                 "Longest video" (the longest one shown that doesn't loop)
                 or one of the scene's videos with its length.
                 Adding a scene picks the longest video that is shown and
                 doesn't loop (a background loop has no end); none: the
                 default duration. Looping and hidden videos are listed,
                 marked "loop" / "hidden".
                 When OBS gives no length (file not loaded, some ProRes
                 .mov), it is read in the file itself (.mov / .mp4 only).
               transition: two badges, its name (FADE, CUT, ...) and its
                 length (0.5s; none for Cut / Stinger). Click the name:
                 Default or one of OBS's transitions. Click the length: a
                 usual length or any other. Grey = the default
                 transition (Settings), blue = the cue's own.
               remove (red bin)
   Last line: scene list + green "+" to add a scene at the end.
   Edits are saved automatically and apply live, even while playing.
   An error of the app (red line in the on-air card) goes away by itself at
   the next scene on air, or with a click on it. The version is at the
   bottom of the dock.

   Playlist files (.obsplaylist): "Save to a file..." and "Load from a
   file..." open the system's dialogs (on top of OBS; on Linux they need
   zenity, otherwise the dock asks for the file / the path). The file is plain
   text (YAML) - it can be copied to another PC, archived with an event,
   sent by mail. Loading never overwrites: a playlist with the same name is
   loaded as "Name 2". Scenes missing in OBS are shown in orange.

Playlist tab (slave)
   No master yet: "Find the master". The network is scanned by itself
   (2 to 4 s): every OBS with its WebSocket server on is listed, OBS
   Playlist masters first. Click the master, type its password (shown on
   the master: Settings > WebSocket server), Connect. The password is
   checked before anything is saved. Not in the list (other sub-network,
   firewall): "Type its address".
   Linked: the master's playlist, read-only, with its progress and its
   ACTIVE / SKIPPED badges (changed on the master only).
   SYNC (bottom of the on-air card, green when it can run, red
   when it can't): a window asks what to copy, one switch
   each - Scenes, Media, OBS settings, All (green on, red off; the last
   choice is kept). In this order:
     1. media: the master's app sends the files its scenes use (no Windows
        share). Same path as on the master; a master drive that doesn't
        exist here: the dock asks which folder to use, and the paths of the
        OBS sources are converted. A file already here with the same size
        and date is skipped; every copy is checked against the master's
        fingerprint; a cut copy resumes. The old version of a replaced file,
        and a file the master no longer uses, go to "<folder> (archive)",
        dated: nothing is deleted. A file OBS is playing here can't be
        replaced: it is at the next Sync. Progress, estimated time left
        ("~3 min 20s left", after a few seconds), speed and a cancel
        button show while copying. A file that was already here (copied by
        hand) is never archived.
     2. OBS settings: canvas and output resolution, FPS, downscale filter,
        colour format / space / range, HDR levels, audio sample rate and
        channels (audio: at the next OBS start, the status says "restart
        OBS"). Never copied: stream service and key, recording format,
        path and encoders, audio / video devices, hotkeys. OBS refuses to
        change its video while it streams, records or runs its virtual
        camera: the status says so and the next Sync does it.
     3. scenes: the master's scene collection opens in this PC's OBS as
        "<name> (from master)" - sources, filters, positions, transitions.
        Positions and sizes are read live from the master's OBS (OBS
        doesn't save a move or a resize in its file right away).
        The output goes blank for a moment; the slave's own collections are
        kept. Missing files and plugins are listed (hover the status).
   AUTO SYNC ON / OFF (next to SYNC, click to switch: red off, green on): the
   slave checks the master every 5 s and syncs
   by itself as soon as its scenes, media or OBS settings change (switching
   scenes doesn't count; moving or resizing a source does). OBS here reloads its scenes then: short blank.
   Several slaves can follow one master; syncing them at the same time
   shares the master's network.
   Master lost (its OBS or its app, for 3 s): notification "Master
   connection lost", the on-air card says MASTER LOST, the slave stops
   following and, as set in Settings > Fallback:
     - "Show a fallback scene" on: the scene chosen below ("Choose a
       scene"), a scene of this PC (none: it stays put);
     - off: it keeps playing the master's playlist from where the master
       was, with its own clock, driving its own OBS.
   Master back for 2 s: it follows again and catches up the master's scene
   at once. The slave
   follows the master live: same scene at the same time, same transition,
   same media actions (play / pause / restart...). A line appears only when
   the link has a problem.
   Versions: when the master and this PC differ, a line under Sync says so
   (the taskbar icon turns orange). Master newer, both on Windows:
   "Update to vX" downloads the master's installer, checks it and installs
   it. Windows asks for the administrator's OK on this PC; the app restarts
   by itself (a few seconds without scene change), OBS stays open, the dock
   reloads. On macOS / Linux, or a master on another system: "install by
   hand" (download the version from GitHub's Releases). Master older:
   update the master. A master installed before 1.3.0 has no copy of its
   installer: install this PC by hand once.

Settings tab (everything is saved as soon as a field is left)
   On a master:
   Default settings  (master only: a slave plays what its master says; if
                     it loses the master it uses the master's last values)
                     default transition and its length (two menus, the
                     same as the playlist badges: usual lengths 0.25s to
                     3s, or "Other" for any length), default scene
                     duration (same menu as a cue's duration: usual
                     lengths 5s to 60s, or Custom) - used by every cue that
                     doesn't set its own, in all playlists; "Restart videos when their scene goes
                     on air" (leave off if the OBS sources already restart on
                     activation).
   WebSocket server  this PC's IP, OBS's WebSocket port and password (what
                     the slaves connect with), show / copy buttons. The
                     password is shown only in the dock of the master PC.
   Tally             see TALLY below.
   OBS Conductor     version, dock port, reachable from the network (needed
     server          on a master) or this PC only, this PC's IP address. Port
                     and network changes apply after restarting the app.
                     "Quit OBS Conductor" stops the app on this PC (with a
                     confirmation). The dock then waits for it.
   On a slave:
   Master            the master followed, its address, the link state,
                     "Find another master". A slave only uses answers
                     sealed by its master with its OBS password: a master
                     older than 2.3.0 (update it), a master whose OBS has no
                     WebSocket password (set one), or another machine at its
                     address is refused, and the dock says which (orange
                     when you have something to do). Scenes still follow.
   Fallback          what this PC does when it loses the master: switch
                     "Show a fallback scene" (on: the scene chosen in
                     "Choose a scene"; off: keeps playing the master's
                     playlist).
   Media location    two buttons. "Same as master": the media go where the
                     master has them (a master drive missing here is shown
                     in orange). "Custom": each master folder and where
                     it goes here ("Choose..." to pick a folder; the cross
                     puts it back to the same path). The folders chosen are
                     kept when switching back to "Same as master". Changing
                     it moves the copies already made at the next Sync (no
                     new download). A folder chosen when a Sync asks for one
                     switches to custom.
   Tally, OBS Conductor server: the same as on the master.

TALLY (ATEM, Roland V-160HD, vMix, TriCaster, Barco E2 / Encore 3,
Analog Way Aquilon / Ascender, PixelHue, Panasonic KAIROS)

A badge centred at the top of the on-air card, semi-transparent and
breathing: red ON AIR when this PC's OBS is on program on the switcher (and
the whole panel is framed in red, breathing too), green PVW when it is in
preview (no frame), nothing otherwise; under it, the name of this OBS's input on the switcher (a long
name scrolls). Red NO TALLY: the switcher doesn't answer.
Settings > Tally, on each PC:
   Switcher      the model. "Scan" looks for switchers on this PC's network
                 and lists them: a click fills the model and the address.
                 To a machine it doesn't know yet, the scan sends nothing
                 but a connection and, for some models, one read request.
                 ATEM and Barco are not scanned: choose them in the list.
                 Not found: choose the model, type its address.
   Address       its IP; the port can stay empty (the model's usual one).
   User / Password   only for the models that ask for one (Roland V-160HD:
                 its 4-digit network password; Aquilon: the Web RCS
                 password; TriCaster: the LivePanel login; KAIROS: the REST
                 API login, user Kairos). The password stays on this PC.
   Input of this OBS   pick it in the list read from the switcher (refresh
                 button next to it); where each input is now is shown.
   Outputs that count   the outputs read on the switcher (PGM, Mix 2, Aux 1,
                 Screen 1...). All count as on air; untick one that must not
                 light ON AIR / PVW (an aux used for recording, a second mix).
Nothing is read from a machine that doesn't say what it is (another device
at that address): the dock says so, and nothing more is sent to it.
"Test connection" connects to the switcher again and says, in green, its
model, how long it took to answer and how many inputs it has (red: why it
failed; reads only).
"Tally assistant" (below the test button) guides you through a short test of
your switcher: connection, model, input names, input on air, in preview,
nowhere, then optional cases (key / overlay, other outputs, switcher lost
and back). You move the sources on the switcher yourself. At the end it gives
you a report to copy, save as a file, or paste in a GitHub issue (it opens
one for you): your password and the switcher's address are hidden, the input
names are in it. It helps get a new switcher supported. Reads only.
OBS Conductor never sends a command to the switcher: it only reads.
The tally is in BETA. It starts CUT each time the app starts: "Turn the
tally on" in Settings > Tally. While it runs, a red STOP TALLY button at the top
of the dock cuts it at once: OBS Conductor then sends nothing at all to any
switcher (OBS and the master / slave links go on). STOP TALLY works even when the
dock is locked.
Not supported: Roland V-60HD / XS-62S (one network connection only),
V-1200HD, older Barco Encore. Details per model: docs/TALLY-DEVICES.md.
If one input is named like this PC (e.g. "OBS HALL 8" on the PC
HALL8: spaces, dashes and capitals don't count), it is chosen by itself.
Empty address: no tally. The multiviewer, a disabled screen or a screen
faded to black don't count as on air. If the tally looks wrong: open
http://localhost:8090/api/tally/debug in a browser on this PC and send what
it shows (what the switcher answers; reads only).

THE ICON (Windows taskbar, macOS menu bar; Linux: no icon, the dock says it all)

Windows: next to the clock (maybe under the ^ arrow). The colour of its play
triangle: green all good, orange something to look at (WebSocket off in
OBS, Sync problem, versions differ), red connection lost (OBS here, or the
master). Pink (the app's colour): master or slave not chosen yet. Hover for the state (role,
OBS connected, scene on air or master followed). Right-click: state, open the dock in a browser,
show the log, quit OBS Conductor (asks first). Double-click: open the dock
in a browser.

macOS: top right of the screen, in the menu bar: the logo with a coloured
dot after it (same colours: green, orange, red; no dot until master or
slave is chosen). Click: state, open the dock in a browser, show the log,
quit OBS Conductor (asks first). No notifications on macOS.

When the app is not running, the dock in OBS says so (Windows: Start menu
> OBS Conductor; macOS: Applications > OBS Conductor; Linux: the
applications menu) and opens by itself as soon as it runs. A web page
can't start a program: starting is done from there or at logon.
Notifications: app started, WebSocket off in OBS, master lost / back,
master on another version.

LIMITS
   - Video length: when OBS doesn't give it, it is read in the file for
     .mov / .mp4 only. Other containers (MXF, MKV...) with "Close file when
     inactive", or a VLC source that never played: set the duration by
     hand. For a VLC playlist of several files, no length is proposed.
   - A Transition Override set on a scene in OBS (right-click) wins over the
     playlist's transition. Playing changes OBS's current transition.
   - Master/slave alignment depends on the network: a few ms on wired LAN,
     more on Wi-Fi. Not frame-accurate (no genlock).
   - The network scan covers this PC's network (a.b.c.1 - 254) on ports
     4455 (OBS) and 8090 (OBS Conductor). Other sub-networks: type the address.
   - If OBS is closed or restarts, the app reconnects on its own; the
     playlist clock keeps running and the scene due is sent when OBS is back.
   - Changes are made from this PC only (its dock in OBS, or a browser on
     this PC). The network can only read: the slaves follow the master, a
     browser on another PC can watch but not change anything.
   - The Windows tray icon is drawn by Windows PowerShell: on a PC where it
     is blocked there is no icon, the app works the same. Notifications may
     show "Windows PowerShell" as their source. Notifications: Windows only.
   - Master and slaves on different systems (Windows, macOS, Linux): scene
     switches, transitions and media actions follow as usual. A Sync copies
     the scene collection as it is: sources that exist on one system only
     (window / screen capture, audio devices, GDI+ text...) are listed as
     unknown on the slave. Automatic updates: between Windows PCs only.
